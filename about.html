About EcoThrive Farms

Learn about our journey and commitment to sustainable farming

Our Story

Founded in 2014, EcoThrive Farms began as a small family operation with a big dream: to revolutionize how people access fresh, organic produce.

What started as a 5-acre plot has grown into a thriving 50-acre sustainable farm that serves over 500 families in our community. Our founders, John and Olaitan, were inspired by their grandmother's traditional farming methods and modern sustainable practices.

Today, we continue to honor that legacy by combining time-tested agricultural wisdom with innovative eco-friendly techniques, ensuring that every product we grow meets the highest standards of quality and sustainability.

Our Mission

To provide the freshest, highest-quality organic produce while promoting sustainable farming practices that protect our environment for future generations. We believe that healthy food should be accessible to everyone in our community.

Our Vision

To become the leading sustainable farm in the region, inspiring other farmers to adopt eco-friendly practices and creating a healthier, more sustainable food system that benefits both people and the planet.
